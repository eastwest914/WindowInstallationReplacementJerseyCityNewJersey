Double Hung Windows

Double hung windows are the most popular window style in New Jersey homes, and for good reason. Both the upper and lower sashes slide vertically, giving you precise control over ventilation and making them extremely easy to clean from inside your home. Our double hung window selection covers a wide range of frame materials including vinyl and aluminum, in countless sizes and finishes to match any home exterior. We carry standard and custom sizes, and our installation teams ensure a perfect, airtight fit every time.

Service 02

Bay & Bow Windows

Bay and bow windows are among the most dramatic and beautiful window installations available for New Jersey homes. A bay window projects outward from the wall with three panels, creating a small nook inside your room and a striking feature on your home's exterior. A bow window uses four or more panels for a graceful curved effect. Both styles flood your interior with natural light, add valuable square footage to your living space and dramatically boost your home's curb appeal and resale value. Our installation teams handle every bay and bow project with precision craftsmanship.

Service 03

Casement Windows

Casement windows are hinged on one side and swing outward like a door, operated by a crank handle. They offer some of the best ventilation of any window type because the entire sash opens — and they seal tightly when closed, making them exceptionally energy-efficient for New Jersey's cold winters and hot summers. Casement windows are also very clean and modern in appearance, making them a popular choice for contemporary and transitional-style homes throughout Jersey City and the surrounding areas. We offer casement windows in vinyl, aluminum and wood-clad frames.

Service 04

Vinyl & Aluminum Windows

Vinyl and aluminum are the two most popular frame materials for replacement and new-construction windows in New Jersey. Vinyl windows are energy-efficient, low-maintenance, resistant to moisture and rot, and available in a wide range of colors and finishes. They are an excellent value for homeowners who want long-lasting performance with minimal upkeep. Aluminum windows are stronger and slimmer, making them ideal for large openings, commercial applications and modern architectural styles. We carry both materials across all window types and can help you decide which is the better fit for your home, budget and climate needs.

Service 05

Sliding & Other Window Types

Beyond our core window types, Home Windows NJ carries sliding windows, picture windows, awning windows, garden windows and more. Sliding windows operate horizontally on a track and are a great choice for wide openings where you want maximum views and easy operation. Picture windows are fixed-glass panels that prioritize views and light above ventilation. Awning windows are hinged at the top and open outward, allowing fresh air in even during light rain. Whatever style, size or configuration you need for your New Jersey home, our team will source the perfect window and install it with the same punctual, professional care we bring to every job.
